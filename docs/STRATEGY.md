# Strategy: market, customers, verticals, roadmap

This is the working version. The published report has the sources.

## Positioning
**"A launch video made from your real product in 15 minutes, from $9, instead of a $300 Fiverr gig or a $5k agency."**

What we can win on:
- **Taste:** brag's creative rules (hook first, show the real UI, no generic SaaS language).
- **Codebase input:** most competitors only take a URL.
- **Recurring videos:** changelog, PR and release videos.

## The main risk
HeyGen, which owns the Hyperframes engine, shipped website-to-video inside its Video Agent in July 2026, with the first video free. Hyperframes also ships open-source `/product-launch-video` and `/pr-to-video` skills, so the core pipeline is copyable. Smaller tools do the same job cheaply: Rendrio ($9 a video), LaunchCut.io, and open-source projects such as Shipreel and gudlab/launchreel. Don't compete on "URL to video" alone. Compete on:
1. Developer and codebase workflows: GitHub App, PR/changelog videos, CI.
2. Brand memory: every video matches your last one.
3. Multi-format bundles: launch video, social cut-downs, App Store preview, share copy.
4. Distribution through the skill's install base (brag has ~8.3K stars and went viral in June 2026).

## Who to target, in order
1. **Indie hackers and vibe-coders** launching on Product Hunt or X.
   - Volume: Lovable sees 100K+ new projects a day; Product Hunt gets ~1,500 submissions a month.
   - Price-sensitive, so sell the $19 single video.
2. **Seed–Series A SaaS teams (YC: ~800 companies a year).**
   - They need a launch video plus a feature video every month. Pro plan, $99.
3. **Devtools and open-source maintainers:** README and release videos straight from the repo. GitHub App.
4. **Agencies and freelancers** reselling at Fiverr prices. Agency plan, white-label.
5. Later: **mobile apps** (App Store previews; ~1M new iOS apps a year) and **Shopify app developers**.

## What videos we can deliver
- **Now:** 15–30s launch, promo and feature videos from a URL or repo. 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1. 7 tones. Music and SFX. Share copy.
- **Next (1–3 months):** changelog/PR-to-video, App Store preview cuts, a social cut-down pack, optional voiceover (Kokoro, Apache-2.0), brand kit memory.
- **Later:** Figma-to-video, personalized demo videos (one template, many prospects), ecommerce product ads, and investor/demo-day reels.

## Customer projections (12 months, estimates)

| Scenario | Paying customers at month 12 | Blended ARPU | MRR | What has to be true |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 300 | $27 | ~$8k | Organic X and Product Hunt only |
| Base | 800 | $35 | ~$28k (~$340k ARR) | Our own PH launch, skill-to-cloud funnel, 4–6% of PH launchers buy (the $9 entry price roughly doubles conversion) |
| Strong | 2,000+ | $42 | ~$85k | GitHub App or changelog wedge works; agency channel |

The launch-video market alone is a serviceable market of roughly $10–25M a year (estimate). Changelog, social, App Store and ecommerce video widen it a lot. For context, Creatify reached $9M ARR with URL-to-ad, and Supademo passed $4M ARR in interactive demos.

## Go-to-market
1. **Every video is marketing.** Free-tier watermark plus share copy that credits the tool.
2. **Product Hunt:** make launch videos for upcoming launches unprompted and DM them to the makers ("we made this for you").
3. **Skill-to-cloud funnel:** a free local skill with a "render in the cloud, no setup" upsell.
4. **No lifetime deals.** Every video costs real tokens, so "lifetime" means unlimited cost. (This replaces the earlier AppSumo idea.)
5. **Agency partnerships**: white-label.
