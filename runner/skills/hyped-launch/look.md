# The look

The target for launch videos: a short film, not a slideshow of screens. Taibanaz's direction (16 Nov), aimed at the
level of Cardboard's sample gallery, with a reference film made for it (`hyped-target-look.mp4`: dark stage, big serif
type arriving word by word, a prompt box, a fan of real videos with depth, cuts on the beat). These rules sit inside the
core's: the format guide still sets the frame, the safe box and the minimum type sizes, and the job prompt wins.

## Stage

- A dark stage behind the product: the brand's darkest colour, or near-black (`#07080a`) when it has none. One soft
  glow in the brand's accent, low and off-centre; a vignette; a light film grain (3–9% opacity) that moves every frame.
- The product's UI sits on the stage as a lit object: rounded corners, a long soft shadow, at most a few degrees of
  tilt. A light-themed UI stays light; the stage around it is what's dark.
- When the brand is light-only and the notes ask for light, keep the same depth on a light stage.

## Type

- Display lines in a serif display face (Instrument Serif is in the fonts), or the brand's own display face when it
  has one. Large: the format guide's headline size or bigger. Tracking about −0.02 em.
- Two to five words a line. Words arrive one at a time on the beat: a rise of at most 40 px and a blur that clears
  (at most 10 px), over 0.3–0.4 s, easing out.
- One accent word per line, in the brand's accent. Body and labels in a plain sans, never more than two faces.

## Motion

- Each scene has one slow push-in (1–4% scale over the scene). Entrances ease out (cubic); no bounces, no spins
  beyond a small settle on the logo.
- Cut on the music's beats (the track's cue file). A cut may carry a 0.12–0.18 s cross-fade; never a dip to black.
- Nothing is still for more than 1.5 s: a word arriving, a cursor, a typed character or the push counts.

## Depth

- Several shots at once (videos, screens, cards) stack or fan with depth: the nearest brightest and largest, each
  step back darker and smaller, and they move at different speeds when the camera moves.
- One focal element per frame. Everything else is darker, smaller or out of focus.

## The end

- The logo lands with a small scale-and-rotate settle, then the name in the display face, then one line, then the
  URL. Held at least 2 s. The music fades over the last 1.5 s.

## Check before you render

From the review sheet: every frame has one focal element; display type is at least the format's headline size; no
frame is still for more than 1.5 s; no dip to black; the accent appears once per line at most.
